Our commitment

Built with sincerity, not for profit

Sunnahway is a dawah project, not a business. Here's what that means for you.

01

Permanently Ad-Free

No banners, no popups, no sponsored content. The app will always be completely free of advertisements, insha'Allah.

02

Your Privacy is Sacred

No personal data is ever sold or shared. We collect only what's strictly necessary to keep the app running.

03

Works Offline

Core features (Quran, Duas, Prayer Times) work without an internet connection. Your ibadah is never interrupted.

04

27 Languages

Bengali, English and Arabic are built in; 24 more download on demand and then work offline. The app speaks your language from the very first screen.

05

Continuously Improved

We release regular updates based on community feedback. Your suggestions directly shape the app.

06

Made for the Ummah

Content is grounded in Quran and authentic Sunnah. We follow the Hanafi madhab with support for all four.
