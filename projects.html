Sunnahway App

Our flagship Islamic companion app: Quran, Prayer Times, Hadith, Duas, Quiz and much more. Free forever, no ads, works offline.

Full Quran

Bengali and English translation, word-by-word meaning, Tafsir and audio recitation. All available offline.

Prayer Times

GPS-accurate prayer times for any location. Hijri calendar, monthly schedule and Adhan alerts.

70K+ Hadiths

Authentic hadiths from Bukhari, Muslim, Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi and more. Daily hadith every morning.

Islamic Quiz

Test your knowledge across Quran, Hadith, Fiqh and Islamic history with multiple difficulty levels.

Dua & Dhikr

Complete Hisnul Muslim with Arabic, transliteration, Bengali and English. Built-in Tasbih counter.

27 Languages

Bengali, English and Arabic built in, 24 more on demand, all working offline. Switch anytime.

Beautiful UI, Smooth UX

Every screen in Sunnahway is designed with care: clean typography, comfortable spacing, and a calming Islamic green palette that makes long reading sessions effortless. Dark mode supported.

  • Distraction-free reading mode
  • Adjustable font size and Arabic typeface
  • Light and dark mode
  • Smooth animations and micro-interactions
