Sunnah Way (“the App”, “we”, “us”) is a free Islamic app that provides prayer times, the Qur’an, Hadith, du’as, Islamic stories, quizzes and reminders. We respect your privacy. This policy explains what information the App uses, why, and your choices. You do not need to create an account to use the App.

1. Information we collect

Location (approximate / precise) Used to calculate accurate prayer times and the Qibla direction for your area. You may also set your location manually. If you deny location, you can still choose a location by hand.
Notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging) A device push token so we can send daily hadith, prayer reminders and occasional announcements. It does not identify you personally.
Basic device info Device model and a “last active” timestamp, used to deliver notifications reliably and to understand overall usage. No contacts, photos, SMS or call data are accessed.
Questions you submit If you use the “Ask a question” feature, your question — and any name or qualification you optionally add — is stored so an admin or our AI assistant can answer it. Providing a name is optional.
App preferences & activity Theme, language, bookmarks, reading position, prayer/amal tracker and similar settings are stored only on your device.

2. How we use information

  • To show accurate prayer times, Qibla and daily Islamic content.
  • To send reminders and notifications you have allowed.
  • To answer questions you submit.
  • To keep the App working reliably and improve it.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not show third-party advertising.

3. Services we use

The App relies on the following trusted providers to function:

  • Google Firebase (Cloud Messaging, Firestore, Realtime Database) — notifications, questions and content sync. See Google’s privacy terms.
  • jsDelivr / GitHub — delivering articles, stories and media (no personal data is sent to them).
  • A prayer-times calculation service to convert your location into timings.

4. Permissions the App requests

  • Location — prayer times & Qibla.
  • Notifications — reminders and announcements.
  • Exact alarms & battery-optimisation exemption — so prayer and daily-hadith reminders fire on time, even in the background.

You can change or revoke any permission at any time from your device settings.

5. Data retention & deletion

Preferences stored on your device are removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data. Submitted questions and your notification token are kept only as long as needed for the service. To request deletion of any data associated with your device, email us at the address below and we will remove it.

6. Children

The App contains Islamic educational content suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond the limited, non-identifying data described above.

7. Security

We use reputable providers (Google Firebase) and reasonable safeguards to protect information. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we work to keep your data safe.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top shows the latest revision. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.

9. Contact us

For any privacy question or a data-deletion request, contact us at:
Email: sunnahway.info@gmail.com
