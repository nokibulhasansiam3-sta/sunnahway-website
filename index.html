Our Story

Born in 2024, built for the Ummah

In 2024, a small group of Muslims (developers, researchers and scholars) came together with one shared belief: that technology and Islamic knowledge, combined with sincerity, can do something meaningful for the Ummah.

2024

The beginning

Sunnahway was founded in 2024 with nothing but a vision and a team of volunteers. The question was simple: what if we could build something truly useful for Muslims, not for money, not for recognition, but purely as a form of da'wah and sadaqah jariyah? That question became Sunnahway.

App

Our first product, the Sunnahway App

We started with what felt most urgent: a comprehensive Islamic companion app for Bengali-speaking Muslims. We built the Sunnahway App from scratch: full Quran with Tafsir, authentic Hadiths, Prayer Times, Duas, Quiz, Islamic Jar and more. Free forever. No ads. Works offline. It was our first step.

Now

A growing platform

What began as one app has grown into a full platform. We now publish free Islamic ebooks and articles by qualified scholars and researchers. Everything is built the same way: sincerely, carefully, for the sake of Allah.

Always

Our commitment

Whatever we build (apps, publications, tools or platforms) will always be completely free. No subscriptions. No advertisements. No compromises on the authenticity of Islamic content. This is not a business. This is da'wah. And we intend to keep it that way, insha'Allah.

Free Forever

Every app, ebook and article, free. No ads, no paywalls, no subscriptions. Islamic knowledge belongs to the Ummah.

Authentic

All content verified against Quran and authentic Sunnah. Scholarly oversight on everything we publish and build.

For All Muslims

Bengali, English and Arabic from day one. We serve the global Ummah. Not a sect, not a group, just Muslims for Muslims.

Fi Sabilillah

Built by volunteers. No profit motive. Every contribution is sadaqah jariyah, a deed whose reward continues after we are gone.
